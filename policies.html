1. How ordering works

  1. Select your products and review the total and any delivery charge shown before paying.
  2. Enter your delivery and contact details and continue to the Revolut payment page.
  3. We check the payment in our payment provider account. Reaching the payment page or submitting an order request does not itself mean payment has been received.
  4. After payment is verified, we prepare the order for dispatch.
  5. Tracking information is supplied when available.
This website uses a manual payment-check process. Please keep your order details and payment confirmation until your order is completed.

2. Shipping & delivery

Processing time

Orders are normally prepared for dispatch within 2 business days after payment has been received and verified. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.

Delivery estimate

The current estimated delivery time is 4–7 days after dispatch. This is an estimate only, not a guaranteed delivery date. Actual delivery may take longer due to destination, carrier operations, customs checks, public holidays or events outside our reasonable control.

Shipping costs and destinations

Any delivery charge and available delivery option must be shown before you place the order. Delivery availability may depend on the destination and product. We will contact you if we cannot fulfil an order to the address supplied.

Tracking and import charges

Tracking details will be shared when available. For cross-border deliveries, customs duties, import taxes or carrier handling fees may apply. Any charges that the customer is responsible for must be disclosed before purchase where required by law.

3. Returns, cancellation & refunds

14-day right to withdraw for eligible EU online purchases

If you are a consumer buying online in the EU/EEA, you generally have 14 days to withdraw from the purchase without giving a reason. For goods, the period normally starts on the day you, or a person you nominate (other than the carrier), receive the goods. Different rules and exceptions may apply depending on the country and product.

How to cancel or request a return

  1. Email support@game-good-now.com before the withdrawal deadline. State clearly that you want to cancel/withdraw and include your order number.
  2. We will reply with return instructions and the correct return address. Do not send goods to an address found by guesswork.
  3. Unless we agree otherwise, send the goods back within 14 days after telling us that you are withdrawing.
  4. Keep proof of postage until the return and refund are completed.

Return postage and condition

For a change-of-mind return, the customer pays the direct return postage cost only where this has been properly disclosed before purchase and the law allows it. You may be responsible for any diminished value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to inspect the goods. Faulty or incorrectly supplied goods are handled under your statutory rights; you will not be charged return costs that the law requires the seller to bear.

Refund timing

Where the statutory right of withdrawal applies, refunds are made without undue delay and no later than 14 days after we are informed of the withdrawal. For goods, we may withhold the refund until we receive the returned goods or you provide evidence that they have been sent back, whichever happens first. Refunds are normally made using the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise.

Exceptions

The right to withdraw does not apply to certain products or situations set out by law. For example, sealed audio/video recordings or sealed computer software may be excluded once unsealed. Any exception will only be applied where the legal conditions are met. This does not remove your rights if an item is faulty.

4. Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, is faulty, or is not what you ordered, email support@game-good-now.com as soon as reasonably possible with your order number and a description. Photos can help us understand the issue.

Consumers retain all applicable statutory rights. For consumer purchases covered by EU law, goods generally have a minimum two-year legal guarantee. Depending on the circumstances and applicable national law, the remedy may include repair, replacement, a price reduction or a refund. We will not use this returns policy to limit mandatory consumer rights.

How ordering works

A simple overview of what happens after you place an order.

  1. Add your chosen product to the cart and enter your contact and delivery details.
  2. Follow the checkout instructions to make payment.
  3. We check that your payment has arrived. An order acknowledgement alone does not mean payment is confirmed.
  4. After payment is confirmed, we prepare your order for dispatch within 2 business days.
  5. We provide tracking information when it is available.
Need help with an order? Email support@game-good-now.com and include your order number. Do not email passwords or full card details.

Shipping & Delivery

Processing time

Orders are prepared for dispatch within 2 business days after payment has been received and verified. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.

Where we ship

We aim to ship worldwide, subject to product availability, destination restrictions and available delivery services.

Estimated delivery

Our estimated delivery time is 4–7 days after dispatch. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date. Actual delivery may take longer due to destination, customs checks, local carrier operations, public holidays or circumstances outside our control.

Shipping costs

Any shipping charge will be shown before the order is completed. Available options and costs may vary by destination and order.

Tracking and delays

Tracking details will be provided when available. If your order appears delayed or tracking has not updated, contact support and include your order number.

Customs and import charges

For deliveries across borders, customs duties, import taxes or handling charges may apply. Any responsibility for these charges will be determined and disclosed in accordance with the rules that apply to the destination and sale.

The delivery estimate starts after dispatch, not when the order is placed. Dispatch takes place after payment is confirmed.

Returns & Refunds

We want the return process to be clear and fair.

Your right to withdraw

Where mandatory consumer law provides a right to withdraw from an online purchase, customers may exercise that right within the applicable legal period and subject to statutory exceptions. For many consumer purchases in the EU, the withdrawal period is 14 days from the day the customer receives the goods.

How to request a return

  1. Email support@game-good-now.com.
  2. Include your order number and tell us which item you want to return.
  3. We will provide the applicable return instructions and return address.

Condition of returned items

Please take reasonable care of items while they are in your possession and package returns securely. Rules concerning opened software, sealed goods, faulty products or other exceptions will apply only where permitted by law.

Refunds and faulty goods

Approved refunds will be handled within the time limits required by applicable law and, where possible, returned using the original payment method. This policy does not limit statutory rights relating to faulty, damaged or misdescribed goods.

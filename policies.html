1. Shipping & Delivery Policy

Order Processing

Orders are prepared for dispatch within 2 business days after payment has been received and verified. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.

Worldwide Shipping

Game Good Now aims to ship to customers worldwide, subject to product availability, destination restrictions and the services available to the destination country.

Estimated Delivery Time

Our estimated delivery time is 4–7 days worldwide after dispatch. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date. Actual delivery may take longer depending on the destination, customs checks, local carrier operations, public holidays or circumstances outside our control.

Shipping Costs

Any shipping charge applicable to an order will be shown to the customer before the order is completed. The available shipping options and costs may vary by destination and order.

Tracking & Delays

Tracking details will be provided when available. If an order appears delayed or tracking has not updated, please contact our customer support team and include your order number.

Delivery estimates begin after dispatch, not when the order is first placed. Orders are dispatched after payment has been confirmed.

Customs, Duties & Import Taxes

For deliveries outside the seller’s shipping country, customs duties, import taxes or handling charges may apply. Responsibility for these charges must be determined and disclosed in accordance with the rules that apply to the destination and sale.

2. Returns & Refunds Policy

We want customers to have a clear and fair process if they need to return an order.

Right to Withdraw

Where mandatory consumer law provides a right to withdraw from an online purchase, customers may exercise that right within the applicable legal period and subject to any statutory exceptions. For many consumer purchases in the EU, the withdrawal period is 14 days from the day the customer receives the goods.

How to Request a Return

Contact support@game-good-now.com with your order number and a description of the item. We will provide return instructions and the applicable return address.

Condition of Returned Items

Please take reasonable care of items while they are in your possession and package returns securely. Any rules concerning opened software, sealed goods, faulty products or other exceptions will be applied only where permitted by applicable law.

Refunds & Faulty Goods

Approved refunds will be handled within the time limits required by applicable law and, where possible, returned using the original payment method. This policy does not limit statutory rights relating to faulty, damaged or misdescribed goods.

Return shipping costs and the return address must be confirmed and disclosed in the final policy before publication.

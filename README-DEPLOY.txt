UPLOAD TO GITHUB PAGES
1. Download and extract this ZIP on your device.
2. Open the extracted spilshop-main folder.
3. Upload the CONTENTS of that folder to the ROOT of your GitHub repository.
4. Replace the existing index.html, styles.css and script.js.
5. Upload/replace all image files as well. Keep CNAME in the repository root.
6. Do not upload this ZIP file or the nested archive into the repository.
7. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish, then refresh the site (on iPhone, close/reopen the page or use a private tab to avoid cache).

IMPORTANT — BEFORE TAKING REAL CUSTOMER ORDERS
This is a static GitHub Pages website, not a full e-commerce backend. The order request is sent only when the customer reviews and sends the email draft. Support must confirm the exact product/model/edition, stock, delivery charge, final total and payment instructions before the customer pays.

Before accepting orders, publish and verify:
- Seller's legal name, business registration/VAT number where applicable, and geographic business address.
- A working support contact and a realistic response time.
- Delivery countries, shipping costs, dispatch estimates and lost/damaged parcel process.
- Clear cancellation, returns/refund and warranty terms appropriate to the seller's country and the customer's country.
- A privacy notice explaining how customer details are received, used, stored and deleted.
- Accurate product condition, edition, compatibility, stock and pricing.

Do not add fake reviews, fake stock counts, false guarantees, or claims that payment is secure/automated unless the relevant systems are actually configured. A proper automated checkout requires a server-side order system and payment integration that creates and verifies the correct amount for each order.

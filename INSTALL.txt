GAME GOOD NOW – CUSTOMER POLICIES

Included file:
- policies.html

How to add it:
1. Upload policies.html to the same root folder as your existing index.html in your GitHub Pages repository.
2. Commit/save the change.
3. The page should then be available at:
   https://game-good-now.com/policies.html

Important:
- This file is a standalone, mobile-friendly policy page in the same dark gaming style.
- It includes the requested dispatch time of 2 business days and estimated delivery of 4–7 days worldwide.
- Shipping prices are not fixed in this policy; the checkout should show the actual shipping charge before payment.
- Before publishing, complete the legal business name, registered address, registration number if applicable, and returns address.
- Confirm that the support email address exists before publishing it as a working contact.
- The wording is a starting draft, not legal advice. Have it checked against the laws that apply to the business and the countries where customers are located.
